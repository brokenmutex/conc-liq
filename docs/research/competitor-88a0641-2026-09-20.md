# Competitor LP reconstruction and $250 inventory-range challenger

Status: read-only chain research and opt-in research/paper implementation. No
signing, funding, service restart, or live-policy change is authorized by these
results. The wallet's reported exceptional profitability is a hypothesis, not
an input to the accounting. No external profitability dashboard was available.

Compact results, source/code hashes and exact transaction evidence:
[`research/evidence/competitor-88a0641-2026-09-20.json`](../../research/evidence/competitor-88a0641-2026-09-20.json).

## Scope and accounting

Wallet: `0x88a0641c1fbff74b99d460c440369f44dd432af9`, Robinhood Chain 4663.
Manager: `0x73991a25c818bf1f1128deaab1492d45638de0d3`.

The capture scans received NFT transfers from genesis to a fixed confirmed
anchor, then reconstructs their complete manager-event histories and wallet
custody boundaries (incoming/outgoing transfers, not later unrelated custody).
It retains receipts, calldata, canonical block headers, pool metadata, NFT state,
and exact pre-action prices: preceding block slot0 followed by any same-block
swap before the action. Public RPC requests/results are cached without endpoint
URLs or environment values. Timeouts split the requested block interval; they
do not become empty successful results.

For exclusively owned, fully closed and collected positions, actual fee tokens
are **core pool Collect minus Burn principal**, including every top-up. Each
nonzero core collection is matched to an actual ERC-20 payment from the pool to
its recipient. Requested periphery collection amounts are not used as payments.
Transferred/open/unsettled positions are excluded from closed-position returns.

Two distinct pool-price diagnostics accompany fee tokens:

- Cashflow P&L: collections valued at their collection-time pool prices minus
  deposits valued at their deposit-time pool prices, before gas.
- Token-flow surplus: total collected tokens minus total deposited tokens,
  valued at the final withdrawal pool price. This is an LP-versus-held-token
  diagnostic, not independently marked alpha or wallet-wide profit.

Wallet swaps, external hedges, other protocols, funding outside these positions,
and historical native-gas conversion remain outside these diagnostics. Gas in
wei is recorded; per-position gas can overlap and must not be blindly summed.

not equally narrow price ranges. Initial allocations in this subset range from
about 6,866 to 638,906 USDG, with many later top-ups. Those sizes do not establish
$250 viability. Median holding duration is roughly five hours, not a strategy
that necessarily recenters every minute.

Using the existing New York market calendar, 41 eligible positions began during
regular hours, 38 during non-market hours, and nine in premarket. Eleven of the
21 associated early withdrawals occurred in non-market hours, eight in regular
hours, and two in premarket. These are action counts, **not session profits**;
positions span session boundaries. This wallet is not an off-hours-only operator.

## A verified behavior worth testing

On September 19, SPCX NFT 1230805 was removed at tick -226024 while its range
[-226040, -226020) still contained the price. Eleven seconds later, consecutive
wallet nonce 1555 minted NFT 1230809 in [-226030, -226020), half the full span.
Neither receipt contains a Swap event. The new mint reused almost all 147.7254
SPCX collected, deployed 35,064.84 of 99,450.09 collected USDG, and left about
64,385.25 USDG undeployed by that mint. This is not a 50/50 reset.

Withdrawal transaction:
`0x9f02aa5886de218fff80c4e4ac2e6ff8c46f2d6966876aeaa2085f2c8a5b441f`.
Replacement transaction:
`0xb668ddd2f3b54b3ef61b7befde0440637ef66a8edc9a42b8ffa8217732ea069b`.

The price changed slightly between the transactions. Reusing the withdrawal
price to simulate the mint produces a different token ratio; the actual mint
price is required for exact reconstruction. Narrowing while withdrawing capital
does not necessarily increase active liquidity or future fee income. These
transactions establish behavior, not the private signal or its profitability.

## Implementation

`InventoryLpReplay` is a separate opt-in challenger, integrated into the adaptive
paper runner through `inventoryRange`. Existing configs retain the original
model. `config/adaptive-paper-inventory-250.json` describes one AAPL paper book,
not a live deployment: 240 USDG inventory plus a separate 10 USDG gas allowance.
Its costs are explicitly labeled historical scenarios, not a current executable
bundle quote.

The challenger:

1. Uses the same initial acquisition machinery and causal rolling 60-minute
   forecast as the controls.
2. Considers full spans of 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, and 32 tick spacings, including
   asymmetric placement. Full spans must not be confused with half-widths.
3. Compares keeping the position with redeploying existing tokens, without a
   swap; excess tokens remain accounted-for idle inventory.
4. Requires forecast incremental terminal value, after the no-swap bundle cost,
   to exceed the larger of 50% of that cost and 25% of forecast new-position
   fees. The same candidate must pass twice; gaps reset confirmation.
5. Enforces a ten-minute cooldown, later-block/time fill, frozen range and
   liquidity minimum, a renewed economic gate, and a reserved full exit cost.

Initial entry and terminal liquidation can still swap. Management moves do not.
The current challenger considers ranges containing spot, not one-sided adjacent
bands. A nearly one-sided wallet can therefore remain inactive for a long time.
It is not a complete market-making controller or a reconstruction of the
competitor's hidden algorithm. Atomic modeled fills still need staged execution
and partial-failure validation before any live use.

Separately, source paper forecast retention now honors configured lookback
instead of discarding data after two hours; startup warmup follows the lookback.
This matters for six-hour configs. No deployed runtime was migrated here.

Decision interval: September 8 00:00 to September 11 19:30 UTC. Earlier retained
events warm up canonical state and the causal forecast. These dates were already
examined by earlier studies: this is an ablation, not untouched validation.
QQQ holdout data is not used. Event-page hashes and final canonical state are
verified. All arms reserve exit gas and compare against their exact post-entry
passive inventory. USDG figures are pool-based modeled amounts, not independently
verified USD returns.

The base challenger averaged **83.01% stock exposure versus 32.24%** for the
baseline. It was **88.99% out of range versus 80.49%**, and its pool-marked maximum
drawdown was **2.87% versus 1.32%**. Its one successful early move widened the
range from 40 to 320 ticks. Higher net return with fewer fees is not proof of
superior narrow-range fee harvesting: inventory path and risk changed materially.
Under stress, its out-of-range fraction was 90.80%, with 82.30% stock exposure.

The NVDA challenger remained out of range 96.01% of invested time and averaged
89.93% stock exposure in the base case. It did not execute an inventory range
move. Positive alpha means losing less than passive, not making money. This
rejects a universal promotion of the challenger; AAPL is only a paper candidate.
Exact post-entry balances/times match across all three arms within each asset
and stress scenario. These controls are not the deployed six-hour/residual
four-book configuration; they isolate the management-policy difference at $250.

No-swap gas is a sensitivity case: the old asset recenter estimate multiplied by
the retained NVDA withdrawal-plus-mint share of the full recenter bundle. Actual
fees use crossing-aware virtual fee allocation with dilution; hypothetical trades
do not alter subsequent canonical market flow. No independent historical
reference marks or realistic multi-transaction execution delays are inferred.
The output's `matchedExecutableAlphaQuote`, not legacy `alphaQuote`, is the
same-entry comparator. Ten USDG is added back only when displaying total account
wealth; it is not fresh trading capital or a second deduction of gas.

## Next decision gates

The useful lesson is to separate **range placement, inventory swaps, and capital
deployment**. Do not copy the competitor's position size, cadence, or smallest
width merely because the wallet collected fees.

- Freeze the challenger in a separate prospective paper state alongside the
  unchanged control; do not overwrite the existing campaign or consume QQQ as
  a tuning set. Label this AAPL selection as retrospective.
- In parallel with that paper candidate, prioritize a verified $250 comparison
  of the competitor's 0.30% SGOV and HIMS pools against our existing universe.
  Check source coverage, independent references, active competing liquidity,
  actual paid volume, token inventory risk and measured exit costs first. The
  successful 1% meme-stock subset is not permission to adopt its risk profile.
- Attribute fees, gas, swap shortfall, inventory returns, net P&L, and matched
  independent-reference alpha separately for off-hours, premarket, and regular
  hours. This replay is 24/7 and does **not** establish an off-hours-only edge.
- Check whether low off-hours volatility is accompanied by enough paid volume
  to cover the complete bundle. Require fresh/explicitly allowed reference data;
  a flat pool price alone is not a reliable stock reference.
- Rehearse withdrawal/collect and delayed remint at the actual $240 size with
  measured costs, allowance policy, exit reserve, custody-safe failure recovery,
  and capped inventory risk. Then freeze a pilot acceptance/stop plan before
  requesting live activation. Do not replenish losses to hide a failed test.

Capture resumes against its saved anchor. Replay refuses to overwrite an output.
Keep the capture directory and its RPC cache; the report is not a substitute for
receipts or source pages. Starting the proposed paper config requires a separate
sealed release and new state through the existing operator workflow, not a
silent migration of an existing book.
