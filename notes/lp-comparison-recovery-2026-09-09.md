# Recoverable LP comparison — 9 September 2026

The forward comparison now distinguishes a temporary data pause from invalid accounting. It stops issuing decisions when source age exceeds 180 seconds, retains its exact portfolio state, and polls for recovery. On return, canonical events reconstruct owned inventory and fees without replaying missed orders. Stale entry/recenter quotes expire; pending safety exits retain their provenance and can execute at a fresh healthy checkpoint.

Both a missing accounting interval and a decision blackout exceeding 900 seconds remain terminal. Processing a continuing stream of stale checkpoints cannot extend the decision-blackout allowance. Changed source identity, revoked checkpoint/current-reference evidence, cost-evidence revocation and failed market reconstruction remain terminal. Failed reconstruction preserves the last accepted ledger and hides terminal economics rather than saving partially credited fees.

Forward decisions now use actual worker time and the shared `readPaperReferenceGate`, with the normal minimum 30-second checkpoint-capture delay retained. Each consumed checkpoint records the actual observation time, whether a decision was made, selected reference evidence and health sample IDs. Historical captures keep their explicitly different checkpoint-only reference evidence; they cannot establish historical current-risk freshness retroactively. Selected current-reference source hashes are checked for later revocation alongside the original checkpoints.

Version-2 state retains pause count, paused duration, skipped checkpoint decisions, last poll time and the last actual decision source. Status files distinguish fresh marks from last-source marks. The available-time ratio measures poll-observed pauses; downtime and checkpoint-decision coverage must also be reviewed. It is not a profitability or statistical confidence score. Terminal invalidity exits with code 2, which systemd reports as failed and does not restart endlessly. Ordinary process failures retain the existing restart behavior.

The previous invalid version-1 cohort is not resumed or relabelled. A new cohort requires a new state filename and pinned runtime. Returns remain modeled market-path outcomes with complete but frozen fork-cost scenarios; this change does not convert historical estimates into fresh transaction-specific cost quotes. The transaction-paper campaign keeps its own conserved budget and benchmark.

Validation: TypeScript and all **279 unit tests** pass. New restart tests cover the recorded 184-second source gap and capture 4.689 seconds after the former stop, fee credit exactly once, inventory/benchmark preservation, stale-quote cancellation, pending-exit recovery, continuous stale-data blackout limits, failed-reconstruction rollback and terminal canonical revocation.
